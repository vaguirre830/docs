Toyota Supra MKV New Horn Install

Tools Needed

  • 13mm combination wrench
  • 10mm combination wrench
  • 8mm socket wrench
  • Philips #2 screwdriver
  • Torx T45 bit
  • Panel Removal / Popping Tool
  • Torque wrench and Breaker bar for wheel removal / reinstallation

Parts Needed

  • Low-tone 415 Hz horn
  • Pigtail wiring harness
  • OPTIONAL: New panel fastener if the Philips fastener broke

Accessing the OEM Horn

The OEM horn is located on the FRONT LEFT side of the car. If you need the most room possible, you need to remove the front bumper. However, you can access the OEM horn through the FRONT LEFT wheel well. This document covers the wheel well access.

  1. Lift / Jack up the car safely
  2. Remove thirteen (13) 8mm screws and one (1) Philips body fastener to remove the fender liner

    (Photo credit to Greg "Chappers" from SupraMKV Forums)

  3. Remove two (2) T45 torx bolts supporting the heat exchanger and move it to the side
    • Depending on your region, the T45 bolts may be in a different mount like this:

      (Photo credit to Greg "Chappers" from SupraMKV Forums)

  4. Remove the two (2) push pin fasteners holding the duct in place and remove the duct
  5. Look in the cavity and you will see the OEM horn. Remove the OEM horn wiring connector
  6. Using a 13mm wrench, remove the OEM nut holding the OEM horn in place.

    Note: there is a washer behind the nut

  7. Maneuver the OEM horn out of the mount and out of the cavity

Installing the New Horn

  1. Connect the new pigtail harness to the OEM connector
  2. Connect one end of the pigtail harness to the OEM horn
  3. Maneuver and loosely remount the OEM horn back onto the OEM mount. Make sure the new horn bracket is sandwiched between the 13mm nut and the OEM horn bracket (see diagram)
  4. Connect the other end of the pigtail harness to the New horn
  5. Mount the new horn onto the new bracket below the OEM horn and loosely secure the new horn with the 10mm nut
    • You don't need to reinstall the washer because the new horn bracket will act as the washer

  6. Tuck away any excess wiring in the cavity so it does not get pinched by the duct
  7. While adjusting the horn so it is out of the duct pathway, Tighten and secure the 13mm nut on the OEM horn.
  8. Tighten and secure the 10mm nut on the New horn. The final assembly should look like this:
  9. TEST - press the Horn on the car and make sure the new tone is audible. You can confirm the OEM horn still works by unplugging the new horn and vice versa.
  10. After testing and confirming the new horn is working, put everything back together in reverse order
