Toyota Supra MKV Android Auto

MMI Installation

  • Follow the Bimmertech installation video on youtube (search for "Supra android auto install")
  • Make sure the dip switches on the unit have the first 3 dipswitches ON (in the down position)
    • This ensures that the unit scales to the 8.8" screen on the supra
    • The Supra 2.0 would only need the first 2 dipswitches on
  • The MMI unit has several connections that are not used. The ones I ran just in case are: USB input and the AUX 3.5mm jack. For the rest, I just zip-tied them and tucked them away.

Switching Interfaces

To switch between the OEM iDrive 6 menu and the MMI interface, press and HOLD the MENU button on the car

Enabling AUX

After installing the MMI unit, you need to 'code' the AUX audio so the sounds works. You may also need to do this if the battery gets replaced.

  1. Open BimmerCode and select Toyota Supra from the list
  2. Select: Headunit -> Expert Mode -> 3000 HMI
  3. Search for CDMM_AUX_SIMPLE_1 in the list
  4. Select aktiv (the default is "nicht_aktiv")
  5. Save and Code
  6. OPTIONAL: you may need to do the same for CDMM_AUX_HU and CDMM_ANALOG_AUX

Once the coding is complete, you can listen to the Android Auto audio by selecting the AUX option in the iDrive 6 menu. Then, simply change to the MMI interface and use your wireless Android Auto
